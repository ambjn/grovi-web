## Getting started

### What is Grovi?

Grovi is an app that turns your game ideas into real, playable mini-games. Describe what you want — "a snake game where the snake gets faster every 10 seconds" or just "something with bubbles" — and an AI builds a working game from your idea, right on your phone.

### How do I create a Grov?

Open the **Create** tab, type your idea in the chat bar, and hit **generate**. You can describe your idea in one sentence, or go back and forth with the AI to refine it before generating. Once the game is built, you can tap **edit** at any time to change something — add a power-up, change the colors, adjust the difficulty.

### Why does generation sometimes take a little while?

Grovi generates a complete, working HTML game from scratch every time. The AI writes all the code, validates that it runs correctly, and automatically fixes any issues it finds. This usually takes 10–30 seconds. You'll get a push notification when it's done so you don't have to wait on screen.

## Credits

### What are credits?

Credits are how Grovi meters AI generation. Every account starts with a free credit balance. Creating a new Grov or editing an existing one uses some credits — the exact amount depends on how complex the game is, because you're only charged for the AI work your specific game required.

### How do I get more credits?

Credit packs will be available for purchase in the app. If your balance runs low, you'll see an option to top up before starting a new generation.

### Why did an edit cost more than a generate?

Editing an existing Grov is sometimes more expensive than creating a new one, because the AI has to read the entire current game before it can modify it. A heavily-edited Grov with a lot of history can be larger and cost more to edit. This is by design — you're only charged for what the model actually did, not a flat fee.

### I ran out of credits mid-session — did I lose my work?

No. If you run out of credits, the current game is preserved exactly as it was. You can purchase credits and then edit or generate as normal.

## Publishing and sharing

### How do I make a Grov visible to others?

Grovs are private by default. Open the Grov's player screen, tap the **...** menu, and choose **Publish**. Once published, it appears on the Discover feed and anyone with the link can play it.

### How do I share a Grov?

Tap **...** in the player and choose **Share**. This copies a link that works in iMessage, WhatsApp, or any other app — the link shows a preview card with the Grov's title and opens directly in Grovi for anyone who has the app installed.

### Can I unpublish a Grov?

Yes. Tap **...** in the player and choose **Unpublish**. It will be removed from the Discover feed immediately. Anyone who already has the link will see a "not found" page.

## Remixing

### What does remixing do?

Remixing creates your own copy of someone else's published Grov. It starts from the same prompt and game code as the original, but belongs entirely to you — you can edit it, publish it, or keep it private. The original creator gets a notification, and your remix is linked to the original in the app.

### Does remixing cost credits?

Remixing itself is free. Editing your remixed Grov afterward uses credits, the same as editing any other Grov.

## Reporting content

### How do I report a Grov?

Open the Grov's player screen, tap **...**, and choose **Report**. Pick the reason (spam, inappropriate content, or other) and submit. Reports are reviewed manually.

### What happens after I report?

We review all reports and take action where our [Terms of Service](/terms) are violated — this can include removing the Grov or suspending the account. We don't share the outcome of individual reports.

## Account and data

### How do I delete my account?

Go to the **Settings** tab and scroll to the bottom. Tap **Delete account**. This immediately removes your profile, unpublishes all your Grovs, and deletes your data from Grovi. This action cannot be undone.

### What data does Grovi store about me?

See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for a full breakdown. In short: your account info (from Google or Apple sign-in), the Grovs you create, and your social activity (likes, follows, remixes).

### Can I export my Grovs?

From any Grov's player screen, you can share the link. Exporting the raw HTML is not currently available in the app — email us at [support@buildwithgrovi.xyz](mailto:support@buildwithgrovi.xyz) if you need a copy of your game code.
