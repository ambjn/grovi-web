## Getting started

### What is grovi?

grovi is a point-and-shoot camera app. Each camera is a complete look — disposable, 2004 digicam, instant square, Kodak-style 35 mm, Y2K CCD, photobooth — applied the moment you press the shutter. There is no edit tab. If you want more, AI Looks re-render a photo into a full aesthetic in the cloud.

### How do I shoot?

Swipe the film strip at the bottom to pick a camera, frame your shot, press the shutter. The top row has flash, timer, aspect ratio and front/back. Swipe **up** on the viewfinder for the pro drawer (exposure, zoom). Swipe **down** for accessories (fisheye, prism and starburst lenses, colour flash gels, double exposure).

### Why does the live preview look slightly different from the saved photo?

The preview is a fast approximation so the camera stays smooth. The saved photo is rendered at full resolution with the real look, so it is the final word. Grain, halation and leaks are only fully visible in the saved file.

### Where are my photos?

In the Roll (tap the thumbnail bottom-left). Photos stay on your phone. Use **Save to Photos** on any shot to copy it into your Photos library.

## Cameras

### Which cameras are free?

FQ R (disposable) and DC 02 (digicam). The other four are Pro, but each can be used free for three shots a day — the "Try today" pill on the film strip tells you a trial shot is available.

### What is the negative?

With **Save original too** on (the default), grovi keeps the untouched capture next to the developed photo. Open a shot, use **Reprocess negative** to change exposure or white balance, and **Develop** again. Long-press a thumbnail in the Roll to peek at the negative.

### How does the photobooth work?

Pick BOOTH, press the shutter once. It counts 3-2-1, shoots, and repeats four times, then prints a strip.

### Can I launch a specific camera from the Lock Screen?

Yes. Create an iOS Shortcut that opens the URL `grovi://camera/k-200` (swap the id: `fq-r`, `dc-02`, `pf-sq`, `ccd-x`, `k-200`, `booth`) and add it to your Lock Screen or Action Button.

## AI Looks

### What happens to my photo when I use a Look?

Only the photo you pick is uploaded, for that job only. It goes to our backend, then to the AI image provider with the Look's prompt. The upload is deleted from our servers as soon as the job finishes. The result comes back to your app, where you can keep it in your Roll, save it, or share it.

### How long does a Look take?

Usually 20–60 seconds. You can leave the screen; it keeps developing and shows under **Developing** on the Looks screen.

### What are credits?

Each AI Look costs one credit. Every install starts with two free credits. grovi Pro includes 60 credits a month; credit packs are available in the app. Credits never expire. If a Look fails or is rejected by the safety filter, the credit is refunded automatically.

### Why was my Look rejected?

The provider's safety filter blocks explicit results. The credit is refunded. Try a different photo.

## Export and sharing

### What is the watermark?

Free exports carry a small "shot on grovi" mark. grovi Pro removes it and unlocks 4K and original-resolution export. Free exports are capped at HD.

### Frames

On any shot, choose **polaroid** or **sprocket** before saving or sharing. Frames are applied to the export only; your original stays clean.

## Purchases

### How do I restore grovi Pro on a new phone?

Settings → **Restore purchases**. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

## Account and data

### Is there an account?

No. grovi uses an anonymous ID generated on your device. Nothing is tied to your name or email.

### How do I delete my data?

Settings → **Delete all shots & data** removes every photo and negative on the device. To delete AI Look records and your credits ledger from our servers, email [support@buildwithgrovi.xyz](mailto:support@buildwithgrovi.xyz).
